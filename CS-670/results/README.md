# Which result tables to use

Tables copied from the archive are numerically unchanged. Their paths, SHA-256
hashes and inclusion reasons are in
[provenance.csv](../reproducibility/provenance.csv). Curated results retain
unfavorable outcomes and seed variability. They are not a new training release.

## Historical baselines

| File in baselines/ | Unit / meaning | Use and caution |
|---|---|---|
| centralized_federated_3seeds.csv | Three matched seed means for B0, B0-UW, B1 | Use this matched subset for the weighting comparison; the B1 mean differs from the five-seed mean. |
| weighting_paired.csv | Three overall paired comparisons | Overall intervals include zero; do not turn point differences into a causal conclusion. |
| final_5seed_means.csv | 34 model/level rows; mean and training-seed SD | Main B1–B4 utility table. `B4_fixeddiag` is a historical fixed-setting diagnostic, **not** E1-FixedB. |
| paired_contrasts.csv | 53 contrast rows, including secondary families | `diff=a-b`; `ci95_*` ordinary intervals; `sim_*` declared multiplicity-adjusted intervals. Use the family column. |
| communication.csv | 10 model families | Dense upload+download payload per participating client-round, rounds and total GB. No protocol/network overhead; no equal-total-budget performance experiment. |
| validation_rank_selection.csv | Five noise/control levels | Ranks selected with validation, not test maxima. |
| privacy_accounting.csv | Twelve historical calibration records | Identify final q=.1,T=50 rows; retained older calibration rows are not extra releases or alternative final guarantees. |
| nonprivate_popularity.csv | Raw train-only counts, validation/test | Historical nonprivate point reference. Not bounded E1 popularity and not DPPop. |
| random_reference.csv | Phase-0 random ranking reference | Sanity floor under the canonical split. |

`nodp`/`nonoise` labels denote the archived no-noise controls; consult the
family's config to distinguish clipping and horizon. B1/B3 converge for many
rounds; B2/B4 use 50 rounds. Blank rank or interval cells mean inapplicable,
not missing evidence to impute as zero.

## Effective-noise extension E1

| File in effective_noise/ | Contents |
|---|---|
| test_means.csv | 59 model/level rows: 54 collaborative combinations and five popularity references; NDCG/HR/MRR means and seed SD. Bounded no-noise popularity has one deterministic score array. |
| contrasts.csv | 117 labeled primary/secondary comparisons. Only `primary_fixed_vs_two` has the sixteen-comparison primary E1 interpretation. |
| rank_selection.csv | Validation-selected Two/FixedB ranks at six levels. |
| selected_rank_utility.csv | **Derived**, 16 plotted rows combining rank_selection and test_means; generated by make_figures.py. It is not the matched-rank primary contrast family. |
| selected.json | Frozen equal-budget hyperparameter selections. |
| accounting.csv | Four iterative collaborative and four one-release popularity calibration rows; the mechanisms are different. |
| geometry_means.csv / geometry_per_run.csv | 54 setting summaries / 270 seed runs; effective matrix/score noise, clipping, norms and stability diagnostics. |
| subgroups_test.csv | 395 descriptive activity/cold/warm rows. Only 14 cold test targets; no multiplicity-adjusted subgroup claim. |
| personalization_validation.csv | 810 seed-level local-user ablations (`own`, `permuted`, `mean`); exploratory validation diagnostics, not privately deployable serving models. |
| synthetic_monte_carlo.csv | Eight numerical moment checks, separate from real-data utility. |
| final_audit.json | Completed E1 audit, including 203 passing tests and historical integrity checks. |

Geometry column interpretation: `D` is the number of perturbed/shared scalar
coordinates. Per run, `q_noise_norm_rms` is the square root of mean squared
Frobenius noise norm over rounds; geometry_means then averages these run RMS
values. It is not entrywise matrix RMS. `score_noise_rmse_mean` averages score
RMSE over declared diagnostic rounds/seeds. `frac_clipped_mean` averages the
fraction of selected clients clipped. `expected_cross_fraction` describes the
conditional expected bilinear matrix-energy contribution. It is not a fraction
of test error caused by that term. Large finite user norms remain included;
corrected final norm summaries use float64.

## Exploratory E2 and E2b

`noise_memory/` contains the raw-coordinate pilot and
`noise_memory_aligned/` its Two-r8 alignment follow-up. Both include:

- `inventory.csv`: one row per probe, 120 and 40 respectively. All have status
  `ok`; paths/hashes identify the existing E1 checkpoint. These are correlated
  probes, not 160 independently trained models.
- `by_seed.csv`: averages over two pulse draws within each checkpoint seed;
  840 and 280 rows. `unit,level,alpha,phase,lag,branch,seed` identify a row.
- `summary.csv`: mean, median, sample SD, minimum and maximum over five seed
  values; 168 and 56 rows. Wide column suffixes identify the statistic.
- `freeze.json`: pre-run protocol/code/input hashes and explicit privacy scope.
- `synthetic_checks.json`: six/two successful synthetic check cases.

`phase=pulse,lag=-1` is the immediate perturbation; `before_reset,lag=0` follows
one exposure round. `after_reset` records lags 0,1,4,10. Branches `retained`,
`shared_reset`, `local_reset` describe the oracle interventions. Alpha is .25
or 1 times the model's own per-round coordinate noise SD. Epsilon labels refer
to the starting states, not privacy accounting for the diagnostic continuation.

`top10_churn_*` is one minus set overlap/10, averaged over users, on catalog
items excluding training positives. Multiply by 100 for percent disagreement;
it is not percent users affected, NDCG loss, or the historical candidate filter.
`pair_flip_*` uses 256 seeded random distinct item pairs. `exposed` denotes users
selected in the single exposure round; it remains that same cohort later.
User counts in seed tables can be fractional because two draws were averaged.

`score_difference_rms` is entrywise RMS of score differences across all users
and items, including training items. `score_ratio_to_pulse` divides by the
initial pulse RMS within the same probe. `direct/local/interaction_score_energy`
and the three inner products satisfy the full score-energy expansion; individual
energies are not additive causal shares. Norm, matrix and closure columns are
in float64 observation arithmetic, while model updates retain original float32.
E2b additionally records maximum alignment product error and rotation size.

`noise_memory/coupling_null_example.json` is **new synthetic evidence**, generated
by the portable coupling-null script after the pilot. It uses no dataset and
checks elementary algebra. It is not a copied E2 training output.

Full per-probe JSON and 2240 unaveraged observation rows remain in the sibling
research archive. See [E2_RESULTS.md](../research/E2_RESULTS.md) for numerical
results and why the coupling control limits the interpretation.

## E3: independent-data replication

`noise_replication/` retains the dataset provenance, source freeze, synthetic
checks and completion audit; validation summaries; and compact shared-reset
endpoint tables. The full 200-probe JSON, 2800 observation rows and all-branch
trajectories remain in the archive. `endpoint_by_seed` has 100 rows covering
20 settings and five seeds. Each seed averages two draws. `top10_churn` is a
fraction, multiplied by 100 in figures. Observers are 128 fixed users per
dataset; training still samples from the full eligible population.

`validation_by_seed` has 40 entries: 30 transferred collaborative runs and
10 separately calibrated DP-popularity releases. `total_bytes` and user norms
are absent for popularity because it has no collaborative training trajectory.
Its `sigma` refers to the single-release Gaussian calibration. ML-1M targets
were scored only on validation. Do not merge these numbers with ML-100K test
tables or portray the diagnostic continuation as having the starting epsilon.

## E4: ranking-tail screen

`ranking_tails/` contains the freeze/check/audit records and compact tables:
200 seed-by-pair rows, 40 seed-aggregated rows, and ten overall screen cells.
All 3200 individual records are preserved in the research archive. Four pair
labels refer to one-based ranks among training-unseen items, without relevance
labels. `exact_risk_numerical` evaluates a known noncentral-chi-square
difference law numerically; its numerical diagnostics are reported separately.
`absolute_error_*` are differences in probability, not percentage points.
`bilinear_share` concerns pair-margin variance, not whole-matrix noise energy.
The predeclared advancement gate failed; no new algorithm was selected.
