\section{How to interpret this supplement}
The main report develops the research questions, methods, and conclusions.
This document retains the numerical detail needed to inspect those conclusions
and the unfavorable results. It is not an additional experiment or an
independent set of replications. All numbers come from the saved source
tables identified in the provenance record.

\paragraph{Privacy labels.} Targets 8/4/2/1 refer to a single trained model
at $\delta=10^{-5}$. ``No DP'' means the matched no-clipping/no-noise control
where defined; ``Clip only'' retains clipping but adds no Gaussian noise.
Diagnostic continuation is not covered by the starting checkpoint's epsilon.
Popularity uses a single full-population release and its own calibration.

\paragraph{Means and uncertainty.} Utility entries show mean $\pm$ seed SD.
SD is not a confidence interval. Paired bootstrap contrasts average each
user's metric over five seeds before resampling users 100,000 times. Their
ordinary and adjusted intervals therefore condition on the retained training
runs. Primary families contain four B3 contrasts, sixteen B4 contrasts,
and sixteen E1 FixedB-minus-Two contrasts. Other families are secondary.
An interval including zero is not an equivalence finding.

\paragraph{Historical names.} ``B4 diag.'' is the historical fixed-setting
diagnostic at common optimizer settings. It is not the E1 model with a fixed
public factor. ``Hist. B4'' in E1 comparison rows identifies an archived
historical comparator. This distinction matters because the naming predates
the fixed-public-factor extension.

\paragraph{Scope of completeness.} All retained main and secondary comparison
rows are included. Geometry, activity, and personalization tables retain
all curated settings. E2/E2b endpoints cover both declared amplitudes and
all retained intervention branches; full time series, seed medians, and
raw observations remain in the research archive. E3's table includes all
20 shared-reset endpoint settings. E4's ten screening cells cover all
50 states and 3,200 pairs. Bulk per-user records, checkpoints, and abandoned
search logs are not duplicated in a printable appendix.

\section{E1 geometry and local personalization}
\subsection{Effective matrix and score diagnostics}
Coordinate dimension, Frobenius noise norm, entrywise score RMSE, and clipping
fraction have different units and meanings. The matrix-noise term is
conditioned on the signal-updated factors; local scale can amplify score
disturbance. Large finite values remain included. The expected bilinear
fraction is not a causal percentage of NDCG loss.

Activity thresholds are at most 22, 23--61, and at least 62 training positives.

\subsection{Validation personalization ablations}
``Own'' retains each user's vector. ``Permuted'' assigns vectors to different
users. ``Mean'' substitutes the mean user vector. These measurements access
internal states and diagnose personalization; they are not privacy-accounted
deployment alternatives. Small differences should not be treated as formal
significance findings.

\section{Pulse/reset and replication diagnostics}
\subsection{E2/E2b endpoints}
Churn is the percentage of top-10 set entries that disagree, not the
percentage of affected users or NDCG loss. A normalized RMS ratio can be
large because the numerator grows or because the initial denominator is
small. Reported means must be read alongside the seed-level and median
records for unstable Two-factor cases. E2 uses all 942 users as observers;
E3 below uses a separately selected sample of 128.

for the prescribed states. The advancement gate requires matched-Gaussian
