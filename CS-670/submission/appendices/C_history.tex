\section{Material revisions and hypothesis decisions}
\label{app:history}
\subsection{Why the project history belongs in the report}
The final tables are not the first outputs produced by the project.
Stopping rules, stability gates, and proposed explanations were revised as
new evidence became available. The purpose of this appendix is to disclose
the revisions that affect interpretation, without presenting every abandoned
grid cell as another independent result. Complete chronological commands,
logs, superseded outputs, and freezes remain in the research archive.

\subsection{Final hypothesis ledger}
\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[Fewer shared noisy coordinates reliably improve recommendation.]
Rejected as a general statement. Four favorable historical private contrasts
coexist with weak-privacy losses, nonprivate losses, and a strong private
popularity comparator.
\item[Fixing one public factor is a sufficient new solution.]
Unsupported by E1 utility, and already preceded by fixed-factor literature.
The implemented method is a controlled baseline.
\item[The factor-noise product dominates the observed failures.]
Not supported by its small measured matrix-energy share. E4 additionally
rejects large Gaussian tail-risk approximation error in the prescribed states.
\item[Noise causes a large general persistent-personalization failure.]
The broad reading of raw Two-r8 distances is rejected by alignment and
no-balancing controls. Modest local-state effects remain.
\item[Factor-basis changes can dominate a paired noise-attribution measurement.]
Supported within the declared simulator, counterfactual, datasets, and
observation rules. Its external consequence and paper novelty remain open.
\item[Public metadata or private ALS has already solved the remaining problem here.]
Not tested. Literature review and comparator planning must not be presented
as completed method implementations or experimental results.
\end{description}

\subsection{What was deliberately excluded from the submission tables}
Superseded sweeps, bulk checkpoints, raw user records, and every intermediate
log are not copied into the submission package. Their omission serves
traceability: the formal tables should have one unambiguous retained source.
Failures and revisions that influence conclusions are summarized above and
in the source inventory. Unfavorable final results, all declared primary
contrasts, and secondary diagnostic families are retained in the supplement.
The source archive remains the complete record of the research process.
