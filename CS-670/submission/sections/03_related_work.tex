\section{Related work and contribution boundaries}
\label{sec:related}
\subsection{Ranking, federation, and private collaborative learning}
BPR learns implicit-feedback rankings by favoring observed items over sampled
unobserved items \citep{rendle2009bpr}. This project uses its pairwise objective
with matrix-factorization scores because the shared item and local user
components are easy to identify and audit. It is a diagnostic baseline rather
than a claim to represent the strongest contemporary recommender. Federated
averaging motivates repeated local optimization and aggregation of client
updates \citep{mcmahan2017fedavg}. Here clients represent users and hold persistent
personalization vectors; the implementation is a centralized simulator of the
federated training protocol.

Gaussian perturbation of bounded contributions is a standard DP construction.
DP-SGD and user-level federated mechanisms establish the relevant methodological
background \citep{abadi2016dp,mcmahan2017userdp}. Privacy accounting in this work
uses numerical privacy-random-variable (PRV) composition, with R\'enyi DP (RDP)
as a separate cross-check \citep{gopi2021composition,mironov2017rdp,mironov2019sampled}.
The single-release popularity baseline uses analytic Gaussian calibration
\citep{balle2018gaussian}. The role of client clipping is itself established:
its effects on convergence and privacy cannot be inferred from the noise norm
alone \citep{zhang2022clipping}.

Private matrix-factorization research predates this project. Private alternating
least squares (ALS) perturbs sufficient statistics for matrix completion
\citep{chien2021privateals}; privacy-preserving federated matrix factorization
has also been developed explicitly \citep{li2022federatedmf}. PrivateRec studies
private training and serving in federated news recommendation
\citep{liu2023privaterec}. These methods differ in objectives, privacy units,
trust assumptions, and evaluation tasks. They motivate stronger published
comparators, but they were not implemented in the completed experiments.
Consequently this report makes no state-of-the-art comparison against them.

\subsection{Personalization and public information}
Local/global personalization has an existing theoretical foundation under
user-level joint DP \citep{bietti2022personalization}. Keeping a user-specific
vector local is therefore neither a new privacy principle nor evidence that
every associated research output is private. The global release, information
used for local prediction, and outputs visible to other users must have the
appropriate scope. Detailed diagnostic access to local vectors falls outside
the training release analyzed here.

Public item features can reduce reliance on private collaborative observations
\citep{curmei2023publicfeatures}. That work is directly relevant to a possible
metadata-plus-private-residual extension. However, E1-FixedB uses an interaction-
independent random basis; it contains no genres or semantic movie information.
It must not be described as an implementation of a public-content recommender.
Work on multi-task privacy under distribution skew also establishes precedents
for heterogeneous task weighting and privacy allocation
\citep{krichene2023skew}. A future activity-aware method would need to account for
that literature and the privacy of any statistics used to choose its weights.

\subsection{Low-rank geometry and fixed-factor methods}
FFA-LoRA already studies a fixed-factor strategy in privacy-preserving federated
low-rank adaptation \citep{sun2024ffalora}. FedASK also addresses limitations of
fixed-matrix approaches and factor-noise amplification \citep{wen2025fedask}.
These are close methodological precedents for E1, even though their application
and privacy settings are not identical to whole-user BPR recommendation. The
algebraic identity involving $AZ_B$, $Z_AB$, and $Z_AZ_B$ cannot supply novelty
by itself. Nor can simply fixing one factor.

The geometry of DP perturbations has wider precedents
\citep{duan2025geodp}. PRISM explicitly studies gauge-dependent low-rank noise
and an intrinsic private mechanism \citep{wang2026prism}. FLoRG uses Procrustes
alignment to address decomposition drift \citep{meng2026florg}; FedRot-LoRA and
FedGSA provide additional neighboring work on rotational alignment and
geometry-consistent federated aggregation
\citep{zhang2026fedrot,zheng2026fedgsa}. The report's alignment control therefore
does not claim a new alignment algorithm. Its empirical role is to test how a
chosen paired-noise construction affects personalized recommendation diagnostics.

\subsection{Noise propagation and ranking stability}
Earlier noise influences later gradients, so a sequence of noisy optimization
steps cannot generally be reduced to independent final-weight perturbations.
NoiseCurve studies cross-iteration noise using model curvature
\citep{gu2025noisecurve}; DiSK develops noise-reduction ideas for private
optimization \citep{zhang2025disk}. These precedents rule out the broad claim
that studying how DP noise changes weights is itself an unexplored direction.
The more specific question here concerns persistent local user state and the
interpretation of paired, factorized trajectories.

Likewise, ranking stability under noisy scores is already an active topic
\citep{urmian2026slate}. Products of Gaussian variables and their relationship
to differences of noncentral chi-square variables have established
distributional theory \citep{gaunt2024product}. E4 applies that theory to a
conditional BPR margin and checks practical relevance. It does not derive a
new distributional theorem or contradict results proved under explicit
sub-Gaussian assumptions.

\subsection{How the literature was used}
The literature review is a targeted primary-source audit, not a systematic
review with an exhaustive search guarantee. The research record distinguishes
full method reading from abstract-level screening. The main audit was completed
through 3 October 2026, with selected bibliographic checks on 5 October. Formal
citation metadata uses 27 retained references. A further screened title with
an unresolved date inconsistency remains in the research search log and is
excluded from the submission bibliography. Preprints are cited as preprints
unless a proceedings source is recorded.

This positioning matters for the interpretation of the whole project. The
established components are BPR, federated aggregation, Gaussian DP, fixed-factor
training, factor alignment, and product-normal mathematics. The completed
project combines them into a controlled recommendation study and a documented
diagnostic audit. Whether the narrower measurement finding supports a research
paper depends on demonstrating a consequence beyond this particular simulator
and comparing it carefully with the closest geometry and alignment work.
